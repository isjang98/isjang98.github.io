FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do photos also go to my gallery?

By default they stay inside SideCam only. You can turn on gallery saving per shutter, or open a photo later and save it to the gallery yourself.

Does it use location (GPS)?

No. The app doesn't even have the location permission. Your parking spot is recorded only through the photos you take, the memos you type, and the parking spaces you registered in advance.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

It's free, with no sign-up or login. Photos and memos are never sent to a server — they stay on your device.

Can auto-deleted photos be recovered?

No. Save photos you want to keep to your gallery, or turn off auto-delete for that shutter. Retention can be set in 9 steps, from 30 minutes to 30 days.

I was using the old Parking Camera app — what happens to my data?

Parking Camera simply became SideCam, so just update. Your saved parking photos and parking spaces remain exactly as they were.

Which devices are supported?

Android 9.0 or later, and iPhone (iOS 26 or later) — get it on Google Play or the App Store. SideCam supports Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified & Traditional), German, French and Vietnamese.
